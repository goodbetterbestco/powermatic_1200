Tech Specs vs Cut Sheet
Our Tech Specs provide technical information to guide product selection, such as materials, dimensions and performance standards. They generally contain information on multiple part numbers associated with that category of products to help determine the best part for your application and to find related and accessory parts.

Our Product Cut Sheets cover a single part number. They are typically used after the system design is complete and the BOM is finalized. The cut sheets only provide high level information and are helpful to include in a documentation package for the system design. The Cut Sheets are helpful to point back to the specific product used in the system if a replacement part is needed or if the system needs to be duplicated.
