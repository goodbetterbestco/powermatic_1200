Estimate method and remaining choices

For panel wires, use the selected terminal positions, horizontal duct centerlines at Y=52, 207 and 412 mm, and the right trunk at X=430 mm. Terminal-block top/bottom ports route to their corresponding adjacent ducts. Add 200 mm per wire for depth, end work and service slack, then round upward to 50 mm. For the four M12 extensions, subtract the retained 200 mm factory pigtail. Local shorting/relay straps use 150 mm, remote switch straps 300 mm, and the S2-to-S3 jumper 500 mm. PE stud and door leads use stated allowances. Add 20% spare to the loose-wire totals and round the purchase budget to 100/100/25 ft.

Confirm the actual external cable routes, S2-to-S3 cable branch, existing E-stop/M12 lead, motor-box connector type, ring bores and grounding studs. Check twin-ferrule fit at K5.3, K1.44, K2.44 and K5.43, ferrule barrel lengths, and final conductor protection/rating before buying the termination variants. The selected gauges are purchasing assumptions.

All 53 connected schematic nets have a complete proposed physical path after accounting for internal duplicate terminals, eight terminal bridges, six reversing-kit links, six direct-mounted overload links, five plug-on suppressors and cable interfaces. All 51 occupied terminal-block wire ports have one conductor each. 102 scheduled wire/core entries use 204 end labels. The schedule assigns proposed physical terminal ports; it does not change the schematic or PCB.

The main BOM was not overwritten. Replace its provisional machine-wire/ferrule rows with the proposed quantities. Retain existing power/motor/control cable rows. Add the heat-shrink label row with supplier GBB and cost $0.00. Ring terminals and chassis-lug rows remain yours to add. Other new prices are blank and marked TBD; the only priced addition in this proposal is the extra top-cover pack, $15 from the saved BOM.
